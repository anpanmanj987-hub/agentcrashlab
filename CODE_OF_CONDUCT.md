# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, impersonate others, or publish private information.
Use synthetic examples and redact sensitive information before sharing evidence.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to keep collaboration safe.
Report a conduct concern privately through an available maintainer contact on the
published repository profile; do not post personal details publicly.
